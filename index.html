Greetings!

I’m Nishi Kanta Paul, a medical AI researcher developing efficient, explainable deep learning for medical imaging and biosignals. I am the Founder & Research Lead of NIMISHES Lab and a Remote Research Assistant at Washington State University under Prof. Asma Jodeiri Akbarfam.

My research spans EEG sleep staging, EEG–fMRI decoding, wearable signal modelling for Parkinson’s disease, diabetic retinopathy grading, chest X-ray grounding and anonymization, and explainable forensic AI, with collaborators including Dr. Jamil Fayyad (American University of Sharjah) and Dr. Md Mostafijur Rahman (Texas Tech University).

I have 10 papers: 6 published or accepted (IEEE BECITHCON 2026, iCONEECT 2026, the MaCVi and FABRIC workshops at WACV 2027, IEEE iCACCESS 2024), 3 under review (IEEE ISBI 2027), and 1 in preparation.

I also work as a Software Engineer II at Brain Station 23 PLC and an Adjunct Lecturer at International Standard University, and graduated from Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (Top 7 in class).

Open to research collaborations: nishikantapaul108@gmail.com
